Sliding-Window Streaming VLMs on Wearable AI

Oct 12, 2026 in progress videostreaminginteractionrecurrent memorylatency

Qwen3.8-27B and Muse Glimmer 30B with an 8-frame sliding window and a self-written memory on all 2,100 Wearable AI validation sessions (EgoLongQA, EgoConv, EgoProactive), run and scored with the official starter kit, with and without the leaderboard's 300 s per-sample rule; EgoProactive is also run under the Pro2Bench paper's protocol.

OverviewEgo-MC-BenchWearable AI

Setup and Protocol

Models, sliding window, memory, serving and the latency study are shared by both benchmarks: see the overview page.

Data and memory refresh

  • Wearable AI (validation split). All 2,100 sessions (the official scorers need all 700 rows per task). EgoConv row 696 has malformed intervals; its turns are answered in listed order, as the starter kit does.
  • Memory refresh (Wearable AI). A separate memory-update call every 2 s of media (10 s for media longer than 10 minutes) and after each decision. Decision times are the benchmark's.

Wearable AI evaluation protocol (official starter kit)

  • Source. The Wearable AI starter kit (identical at our pinned revision and current main). There is no benchmark paper with baselines for the three tasks, so the comparison points are the validation leaderboard and, for EgoProactive, the Pro2Bench paper.
  • Decisions and prompts. Official prompts and history helpers are imported from the starter kit. EgoLongQA: one question after the video, no system prompt. EgoConv: turn i is answered when its interval ends, with the model's own earlier answers as history. EgoProactive: a decision at the end of every ~8 s chunk, with the query and the last 4 turns of the gold dialogue as history, answering $interrupt$ … or $silent$.
  • Our variable. The starter kit samples frames from the video; we give the 8 most recent frames plus the model's own memory, placed where the starter kit puts its frames. Output budget 4,096 tokens per call (the starter kit's 16 / 512 are baseline defaults, not rules).
  • Scoring. The starter kit's run_evaluation.py, unmodified: EgoLongQA accuracy, EgoConv BLEU-4 and the Llama-4-Maverick LLM judge (the leaderboard metric), EgoProactive macro and G-mean F1.
  • 300 s rule. The leaderboard allows 300 s of compute per sample. We estimate each call's isolated single-stream H200 time from its token counts (first-token time plus decode speed from the latency study); a sample over 300 s is exported as empty. Scores are reported with and without the rule.
  • Pro2Bench protocol (EgoProactive only). The benchmark paper (arXiv 2606.04970, App. G, No-Plan condition): its system prompt and duplex user prompt verbatim, 8 frames per chunk, G-mean F1. The visual summary slot holds our memory; the paper's PQS needs an LLM judge and is not computed.

System prompt App. G.2, verbatim
You are a helpful, proactive AI assistant guiding a user through a step-by-step procedural task. You observe the user’s activity through egocentric video from their perspective.

Your responsibilities:
1. Track which step the user is currently performing.
2. Detect when a step is completed and proactively announce the next step.
3. Detect mistakes or deviations from the expected procedure:
 - Skipped steps
 - Wrong step ordering
 - Technique errors
 - Substitutions or improvisations
4. When a deviation is detected, suggest a recovery action.
5. If no guidance is needed (user is progressing normally), stay silent.

Output format:
- If the user needs guidance (step completed, mistake detected, or question asked), respond with: $interrupt$ followed by your guidance.
- If no guidance is needed, respond with exactly: $silent$

Be concise. Focus on what the user should do next, not what they just did.

Observations

  • On EgoProactive the operating point depends on the prompt. with the starter-kit prompt Muse interrupts in 98 % of chunks (silent recall 0.02) and Qwen is balanced (0.40 / 0.59); with the Pro2Bench prompt Muse is the balanced one (0.76 / 0.35) and Qwen almost never speaks (interrupt recall 0.06).
  • Memory carries long videos better for Qwen. EgoLongQA questions arrive after a 10-minute stream, so the answer must come from the 512-token memory. With the official "answer with only the letter" prompt Muse no longer abstains (our earlier prompt: 0.31) and reaches 0.52; Qwen reaches 0.66, inside the 0.59–0.69 range of the zero-shot Qwen2.5-VL-7B leaderboard entries (best entry 0.96).
  • Free-form answers still need the judge. EgoConv BLEU-4 is low for both models (0.063 vs 0.048) and says little about correctness; the leaderboard's LLM judge is queued. Qwen's 95 failed turns are empty answers (its first token was end-of-turn).
  • The 300 s budget is the binding constraint. Refreshing the memory every 2 s means hundreds of calls per sample. Estimated on an idle H200, Muse exceeds 300 s on 699 of 700 EgoLongQA samples and its scores under the rule drop to about zero; Qwen keeps 0.41 EgoLongQA accuracy and 0.24 EgoProactive macro F1.

scripts/serve_and_run.sh muse30b 1 32 configs/wearable_official_klone.yaml   # same for qwen27b
