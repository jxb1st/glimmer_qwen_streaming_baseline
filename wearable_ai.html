Sliding-Window Streaming VLMs on Pro2Bench (EgoProactive)

Oct 12, 2026 in progress videostreaminginteractionrecurrent memorylatency
EfficiencyEgo-MC-BenchPro2Bench

Setup

  • Data: EgoProactive validation split of Wearable AI, 700 sessions, 9,935 chunks of ~8 s, each labelled interrupt or silent.
  • Task: at the end of every chunk, $interrupt$ <guidance> or $silent$.
  • Protocol: No-Plan zero-shot condition of the Pro2Bench paper (App. G); the user message holds the chunk's 8 frames, our memory as the visual summary, the user's request and the last gold assistant reply; greedy decoding for all models.
  • Frame rate / window: 1 fps; the last 8 frames (8 s, long edge ≤ 672 px) plus a self-written memory, instead of the whole past video.
  • Qwen3.8-27B: thinking off and thinking low (reasoning_effort=low).
  • Muse Glimmer 30B: reasoning_strength=low; reported on SGLang + DFlash, plain-vLLM run also shown.

Metrics

  • G-Mean F1: each chunk is a two-way decision (interrupt or stay silent); F1 is computed for each class and the two are multiplied and square-rooted, so always interrupting or always staying silent scores 0.
  • Interrupt / silent recall: share of chunks that should get an interruption that do, and share of chunks that should stay silent that do.
  • PQS / guidance quality (paper only): an LLM judge scores what is said in correctly fired interrupts; not computed for ours.

Prompts

System prompt App. G.2, verbatim
You are a helpful, proactive AI assistant guiding a user through a step-by-step procedural task. You observe the user’s activity through egocentric video from their perspective.

Your responsibilities:
1. Track which step the user is currently performing.
2. Detect when a step is completed and proactively announce the next step.
3. Detect mistakes or deviations from the expected procedure:
 - Skipped steps
 - Wrong step ordering
 - Technique errors
 - Substitutions or improvisations
4. When a deviation is detected, suggest a recovery action.
5. If no guidance is needed (user is progressing normally), stay silent.

Output format:
- If the user needs guidance (step completed, mistake detected, or question asked), respond with: $interrupt$ followed by your guidance.
- If no guidance is needed, respond with exactly: $silent$

Be concise. Focus on what the user should do next, not what they just did.
User message after the chunk's 8 frames; No-Plan duplex template, visual summary = our memory
Here is the summary of the recent visual context: {visual_summary}
Here is the most recent conversation between the user and the assistant:
User: {user_utterance}
Assistant: {assistant_response}
Memory update ours, a separate call; [context] = no question yet / turns so far / the user's query
You are watching a live first-person video but only ever see the most recent frames (each labeled with its time in seconds) plus a short written memory that you yourself wrote on the previous update. Nothing else about earlier footage survives, so the memory must carry everything you may need later: what the person did and where, objects and their locations, text you saw, counts, the current state, and what was said.
Context so far: [context]
Hard limits: observed_facts at most 8 entries, completed_steps at most 6, pending_commitments at most 3, uncertainties at most 3; every entry at most 15 words and prefixed with its time (e.g. "12s: ..."); current_state at most 40 words. Merge or drop older entries instead of exceeding these limits. Describe only what the frames support.
Reply with ONE JSON object and nothing else, in exactly this shape:
{"observed_facts": [...], "completed_steps": [...], "current_state": "...", "pending_commitments": [...], "uncertainties": [...]}

Takeaways

  • Qwen thinking low is the best zero-shot model here: G-Mean F1 0.635, above every zero-shot result in the paper (best 0.62, Claude Opus 4.6) and second only to the paper's fine-tuned planner system (0.68).
  • Thinking decides whether Qwen speaks: with thinking off it stays almost always silent (interrupt recall 0.06, G-Mean 0.26); with thinking low it is balanced (0.58 / 0.70).
  • Muse leans towards interrupting: G-Mean 0.53–0.54, interrupt / silent recall 0.69–0.76 / 0.35–0.41; the serving stack barely changes it.
  • Only timing is scored for ours: the quality of what is said (PQS) needs the paper's judge, and we ran one prompt where the paper reports the best of three.
