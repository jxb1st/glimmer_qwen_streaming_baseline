Observations and Next Steps

  • Neither model is a usable proactive assistant yet: each is strong on one half of the task.
  • A fixed 8-frame window holds up over full recipes, where the paper's models degrade.
  • The 300 s/sample budget binds on plain vLLM (Muse over budget on 699/700 LongQA samples); with DFlash only 59/700.
  • On plain vLLM Muse skipped reasoning on EgoProactive decisions (median 3 tokens) and interrupted 98 % of the time; natively it reasons (112) and stays silent more often. Cause under investigation.

Next

  • Find why plain vLLM made Muse skip reasoning on EgoProactive (render the same request on both engines).
  • Give Qwen its own official speed-up; run Qwen thinking (low) through the full benchmarks.
  • Multiple Muse seeds; whole-video baseline; EgoConv LLM judge for Qwen and native Muse (queued); Pro2Bench PQS.
