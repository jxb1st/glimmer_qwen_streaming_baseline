Qwen3.8-27B and Muse Glimmer 30B as causal online assistants (8-frame sliding window + self-written memory) on Ego-MC-Bench and Wearable AI under each benchmark's official protocol, with an isolated latency study that includes Muse on its native serving stack (SGLang + DFlash).

  • Opposite operating points. Muse catches mistakes (Ego-MC F1 0.29, best incl. the paper); Qwen confirms steps (IC-Acc 44).
  • The prompt decides EgoProactive. Muse leads with the Pro2Bench prompt (0.53, paper best 0.57), Qwen with the starter kit.
  • Serving matters. DFlash makes Muse 2.3× faster to decode (3.1 s per call); no setup meets a 2 s refresh cadence.

Observations and Next Steps

  • Neither model is a usable proactive assistant yet: each is strong on one half of the task.
  • A fixed 8-frame window holds up over full recipes, where the paper's models degrade.
  • The leaderboard's 300 s/sample budget is the binding constraint on plain vLLM.
